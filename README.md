India is one of the largest agricultural producers globally. However, small and marginal farmers often lack access to scientific on-farm storage facilities. 
As a result, they rely on private warehouses for storing grains such as wheat, paddy, and maize.

The current system suffers from:
Paper-based warehouse receipts
Duplicate or fake receipts
Lack of transparency
High storage charges
Multiple loans issued on the same stock
Delayed payment & distress sales

This project proposes a Blockchain-Based Electronic Warehouse Receipt (e-WR) System to eliminate fraud, increase transparency, and empower farmers through secure and tamper-proof digital records.
